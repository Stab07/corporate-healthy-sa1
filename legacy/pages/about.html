About Corporate Healthy SA

Est. 2007

A Holistic Approach to Employee Health

Corporate Healthy SA is a wellness organization comprising a network of health practitioners and service providers. We offer a holistic approach to employee health and wellness and have been operating since January 2007.

We are proud to be the only wellness company in South Africa that offers wellness services in conjunction with specialist service providers - optometrists, dieticians, audiologists and more. Our nurses specialize in health risk assessments, voluntary counselling & testing (VCT), and medical surveillance.

Beyond health screening and nursing, we offer stress management and team-building activities.

Our Purpose

Periodic Health Screenings Save Lives

All employees should have periodic health screenings as part of routine primary care, especially since many diseases are silent or asymptomatic. Employees with undetected health problems reduce efficiency and increase the risk of workplace accidents. We believe prevention is always better than cure.
